# Brand voice

> Starter version — Roy to refine. This whole file is sent to Claude on every draft.

## Who's talking
Roy: a UK software engineer who builds automations for small businesses. Writes like a
practical tradesperson explaining a job, not a marketer or an AI enthusiast.

## Tone
- Direct, warm, a bit dry. Confident without hype.
- Talks *to* one owner ("you"), not "businesses" in the abstract.
- Specific over clever: name the actual task (answering the phone mid-appointment,
  chasing no-shows, copying bookings between systems).
- Short sentences. Short paragraphs. Lots of white space on LinkedIn.

## Avoid
- Hype words: "game-changer", "revolutionise", "unlock", "supercharge", "leverage".
- Jargon: RAG, agentic, LLM, GPT, workflows-as-a-service, orchestration.
- Emoji walls and hashtag stuffing (max 3 hashtags on LinkedIn, none on Facebook).
- Fake urgency, made-up numbers, invented clients.

## LinkedIn format
- First two lines are the hook — they must work on their own before "…see more".
- 120–250 words. One idea per post.
- External links go in the first comment, never in the body.
- End with a soft CTA (e.g. "Comment PHONE and I'll send you the checklist",
  "DM me 'calls' if you want to see how this would work in your salon").

## Facebook format
- Shorter (50–120 words), chattier, more like talking to a local business owner.
- Different wording from the LinkedIn version — never the same copy.
- A question at the end to invite comments.
